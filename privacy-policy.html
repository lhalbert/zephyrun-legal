ZephyRun

Privacy Policy

Last reviewed August 26, 2026 · Applies to the ZephyRun iOS and watchOS apps

ZephyRun builds your training plan from runs you log — on your phone, on your wrist, or in any other app that writes to Apple Health. Here's exactly what that involves, in plain language.

On-device only No ads No data sold No account required

What we read from Apple Health

Your past runs — distance, duration, and heart rate — to estimate your fitness and set training paces.

GPS routes from runs logged in other apps, so Run Detail can show a map and splits.

What we write to Apple Health

A summary of each run you record with ZephyRun — distance, duration, and route where available.

Location

ZephyRun uses your location only while you're actively recording a run, to calculate pace, distance, and route.

What we store on your device

Your training plan and preferences (units, notifications). There's no account or login.

What we never do

Your health data stays on this iPhone. There's no backend server in ZephyRun — nothing is uploaded.

We never sell your data or share it with third parties, and never use it for advertising.

Instrumentation (app usage patterns, visible in Settings) is logged locally on-device only — it's never transmitted anywhere.

Your controls

Export your run history or delete all local app data anytime from Settings → Data & privacy.

Children

ZephyRun isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same page and reflected in the app's own Settings → Privacy Policy screen.

ZephyRun Health disclaimer: ZephyRun is a training-plan tool, not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice.
