ZephyRun

A dad built a training app so his daughter could run her first half marathon.

She finished. Now ZephyRun is on the App Store for every new runner.

HARRISONBURG HALF MARATHON 13.1 MI · FINISHER OCT 3, 2026 · HARRISONBURG, VA

The story

When his daughter Ashlynn, a JMU graduate student in Harrisonburg, signed up for the Harrisonburg Half Marathon, Louis Halbert, a Virginia veteran and software developer, built her a training app that gives runners a real plan without a subscription or an account.

ZephyRun builds a personalized plan from the race distance and date, sets easy, tempo and interval paces from the runner's current fitness, and adjusts when life gets in the way. Ashlynn trained with it and finished her first half marathon on October 3, 2026. The app is now free to download on the App Store.

What it does

  • Builds a training plan for a specific race and date, with paces grounded in recent runs from Apple Health.
  • Adapts the plan to missed runs, race-date changes and how many days a week the runner wants to train.
  • Tracks runs live with pace, distance and heart rate on iPhone or Apple Watch.
  • Works strength training into the week without crowding hard running days.
  • Counts every run in Apple Health, including runs logged in other apps.
